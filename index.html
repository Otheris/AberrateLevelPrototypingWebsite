Welcome to the Level Editor

Here is a quick overview of how to use the editor:

  • Draw Rooms: Use the Rooms tool to draw the layout of your puzzle.
  • Add Entities: Use the Entities tool to place objects like buttons, actuators, and cubes.
  • Add Logic: Select entities to configure their properties and connect logic in the settings panel.
  • Play Mode: Click the Play button in the top right to simulate and test your puzzle.
  • Export: Name your level at the top and export it.
  • Cube Types & Recipes: Click the Cube Types or Recipes buttons at the top to customize weights, colours, and fusion rules.

Discord Sharing: Remember to input your Discord webhook links via the '+' button next to Export so you can easily share your puzzles with the team!
